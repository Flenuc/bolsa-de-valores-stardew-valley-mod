# Changelog / Historial de cambios

## 1.0.0 — primera versión pública / first public release

- **Commodities**: 15 commodities with prices driven by supply (your sales), seasons, noise and events; inflation (IPC-F).
- **Taxes (Argentine style)**: Monotributo → Responsable Inscripto, IVA, Ingresos Brutos, Seguridad e Higiene, ABL,
  Ganancias with advances; late payment interest, notices and collections; pay at Lewis's town ledger, at the Agency
  or from the Terminal; auto debit.
- **Stock exchange (BYMZ)**: 10 fictional companies, MERZU index, Treasury bills, repos (cauciones), sovereign and
  corporate bonds (fixed, floating, CER), credit ratings, defaults and restructurings, BCRF policy rate.
- **Derivatives (MATZU)**: commodity futures with margin and physical delivery through the Storage Silo; stock
  options with collateral.
- **Debts**: attachments, insolvency proceedings, bankruptcy (optional building auction in Argentina), bank loans,
  repo borrowing and your own credit rating.
- **Your companies**: farming, mining, fishing, industrial and trading companies (LLC or corporation) with their own
  books and taxes; export to Gotoro with export duties; issue your own bonds and take your corporation public (IPO).
- **Stock Agency** in town with **Julián Ferreyra**, your stockbroker: schedule, market-aware dialogue, gifts, three
  heart events and a friendship commission discount. Compatible with Stardew Valley Expanded.
- **Gotoro gold**: exchange rate in every difficulty; in Argentina, currency controls, MEP, black market at Krobus's
  and devaluations.
- Three difficulty levels (Casual, Realista, Argentina), Spanish and English, Generic Mod Config Menu support.
